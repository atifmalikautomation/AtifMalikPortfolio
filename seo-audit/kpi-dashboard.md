## Metric Definitions

### A. Indexed Pages

| Source | How to Check |
|--------|-------------|
| Google Search Console | Coverage > "Valid" pages count |
| Bing Webmaster Tools | Site Explorer > "Indexed Pages" |

**Target:** All published pages indexed (currently ~15–20 pages). Zero "Excluded" pages that should be indexed.

### B. Impressions & Clicks by Keyword Map

Track in GSC > Performance > Search Results. Filter by the following keyword clusters:

| Keyword Cluster | Example Queries |
|----------------|-----------------|
| AI video production Pakistan | ai video editor pakistan, ai commercial production, ai video freelancer |
| AI video ads / commercials | ai product video, ai commercial maker, ai ad production |
| n8n automation agency | n8n developer, n8n automation freelancer, workflow automation agency |
| AI chatbot development | ai chatbot developer, custom chatbot freelancer, ai chatbot agency |
| AI content creation | ai youtube video production, ai social media content |
| Atif Malik (branded) | atif malik, atifmalik.me, atif malik ai |

### C. AI Referral Sessions

Track in your analytics tool (Vercel Analytics, Google Analytics, or Plausible). Filter referral source by regex:

```
chatgpt\.com|chat\.openai\.com|perplexity\.ai|copilot\.microsoft\.com|gemini\.google\.com|you\.com|poe\.com|claude\.ai|phind\.com
```

**What to record:**
- Total sessions from AI referrals
- Top AI referrer by volume
- Landing pages receiving AI traffic

### D. Bing WMT AI Performance Citations

- Go to Bing Webmaster Tools > AI Performance (if available for your site)
- Record: number of citations, which pages are cited, which queries triggered citations

### E. Bookings from Organic / AI

- Track form submissions or booking link clicks
- Tag source: organic search, AI referral, direct, social, paid
- Record total bookings and conversion rate (bookings / sessions)

### How to Record Results

For each prompt, in each AI tool, record:

| Field | Values |
|-------|--------|
| **Mentioned?** | Yes / No — Was Atif Malik or atifmalik.me mentioned by name? |
| **Cited?** | Yes / No — Was atifmalik.me shown as a source/citation link? |
| **Linked?** | Yes / No — Was a clickable link to atifmalik.me provided? |
| **Position** | 1st, 2nd, 3rd... or "not listed" — Where in the response did the mention appear? |
| **Notes** | Any relevant context (e.g., "mentioned among 5 others", "was the top recommendation") |
